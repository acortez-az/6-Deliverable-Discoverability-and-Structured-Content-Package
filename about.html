About the Game

Welcome to Your New Life!

Have you ever dreamed of becoming the mayor of your town? In Animal Crossing: New Leaf Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, you aren't just a resident, you're the town's newly appointed Mayor!

Whether you want to fish by the ocean, design custom clothing patterns, decorate your house, or build public works projects, your village grows at your own pace.

Key Features for New Players

You're the Mayor!

Pass ordinances to change town rules, build bridges, benches, and lamp posts to shape your town into a dream destination.

Island Getaways

Take a boat ride with Kappn' to Tortimer Island, a tropical resort where you can play fun mini-games and catch rare exotic bugs and fish.

Custom Designs

Express your creativity by designing custom patterns for clothing, paths, and furniture, or share them with friends using QR codes!

Get to know your Villagers

With over 300 unique villagers to befriend, trade items, celebrate their birthdays, and watch them move in or out of your village.

Wireless broadband Internet access and a Nintendo Online Membership required for online features. For more info, go to support.nintendo.com.
